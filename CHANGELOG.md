# Changelog

## 0.3.2

- Crafting table: furniture products show measured numbers again on the new workbench screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.3.1

- After a game update, a hook that can no longer be placed is logged as an error, so the Mod Manager can mark the mod as maybe needing an update.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19131.

## 0.3.0

- The Mods screen links this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, settings that only matter while another setting is on stay hidden until it is.
- On the Mods screen, TraceLog sits in the folded Advanced group.
- ApplyModifiers changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen takes effect at once. Enabled, Measure and TraceLog take effect at the next game start.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings changed on the Mods screen take effect without a restart; without it, at the next game start.

## 0.2.2

First public release. See the numbers behind the + and - marks in Survival Log's action tooltips: -10 satiety instead of two minus signs. Measured from what your character really got once you have used an action, the game's ~estimate until then.
