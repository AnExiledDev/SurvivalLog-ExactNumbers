# Changelog

## 0.3.4

- Less hitch on the first object menu you open after starting the game.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.3.3

- Less stutter: saving the numbers the mod has measured no longer pauses the game for a moment.
- The first menu you open after starting the game no longer makes the game hitch.
- Menus and tooltips get their numbers with less work, so the mod costs the game less while you play.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.3.2

- Fixed: furniture on the new workbench screen shows measured numbers again.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.3.1

- After a game update, Mod Manager can tell you if this mod may need an update.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19131.

## 0.3.0

- Mod Manager's Mods screen links to this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, a setting that only matters while another one is on stays hidden until that one is on.
- On the Mods screen, TraceLog is in the Advanced group, which starts folded.
- A change to ApplyModifiers on the Mods screen takes effect at once. Enabled, Measure and TraceLog take effect the next time you start the game.
- Mod Manager's Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings you change on the Mods screen take effect right away. Without it, they take effect the next time you start the game.

## 0.2.2

- First public release.
- Action tooltips show numbers instead of + and - marks: -10 satiety instead of two minus signs.
- Once you've used an action, the number is what your character actually got. Until then it's the game's estimate, marked with ~.
